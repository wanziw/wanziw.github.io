前三篇比较重要

- Passalacqua & Gillis, Nature Plants, 2024 — *Coexpression enhances cross-species integration of single-cell RNA sequencing across diverse plant species*
  - 从 many-to-many orthology 出发，用 **coexpression** 找 proxy gene pairs
- Haghani et al., Journal of Experimental Botany, 2026 — *Cross-species plant single-cell analysis: community challenges and shared solutions*

> One promising direction is a scalable framework that combines heterogeneous computational strategies with a curated, continuously expandable reference atlas ([Fig. 3](javascript:;)). Such a system could reduce annotation inconsistencies by grouping genes into ‘macrogenes’ derived from **orthogroups, protein language models, co-expression profiles, or DNA foundation-model embeddings.** Different macrogene definitions and integration strategies could be systematically benchmarked to identify the most robust combination for cross-species label transfer. The resulting annotations would assign cell type identities while quantifying confidence, enabling detection of conserved versus potentially novel states, and the reference atlas could be updated iteratively as new datasets are incorporated.

- Orthologous marker groups reveal broad cell identity conservation across plant single-cell transcriptomes 2025

- Nature, 2024 — *Exaptation of ancestral cell-identity networks enables C4 photosynthesis*
- Rosen et al., Nature Methods, 2024 — SATURN, *Toward universal cell embeddings: integrating single-cell RNA-seq datasets across species with SATURN*
